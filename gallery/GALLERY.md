# PHerc1451: six largest v3 candidate surfaces

Pictures of the six largest surfaces in this release. Surface geometry and CT intensity only. No ink result or visible-text claim. Winding identity is not independently verified.

Source: the surfaces in this archive (`surfaces/gated/`; built from the staged archive SHA256 `8d05c067...` before this gallery was added). Six largest units selected by `area_in_file_cm2` in its `INDEX.csv`; an additional known limitation is shown below.

**READ gate status:** these six units pass the fibre check and self-check, and the winding cross-check where they overlap (release notes). This does not establish a single winding outside those overlaps. Areas below are per-file areas, not additive de-duplicated area.

**How to read:** flattened views sample CT on the released bilinear parameter grid; dark blue is missing support. Their grid axes are not an isometric flattening. Gold lines mark the three chosen section rows. Each overview states its CT level: L4 is 38.384 um and L3 is 19.192 um; fixed central L1 insets use 4.798 um CT. Overviews are coarse intensity, not resolved-fibre claims; insets are intensity texture, not ink predictions. Each section shows the full longest continuous row run and a fixed midpoint detail, with green kept support and magenta removed support; full-row horizontal scale is compressed.

**Inherited coordinate caveat:** The cross-section strips used `L1 = 2*L2`, whereas canonical voxel-centre conversion adds 0.5 in each axis. Their scientific rasters are reused unchanged and sample -0.5 L1 voxel in x/y/z. The saved trace vertices match the archive (measuRED, `checks.json`). New flattened images use canonical voxel-centre coordinates. Each reused strip retains its original per-part display stretch.

## Offset control

![Offset control](offset_control.png)

Measured from saved raw CT samples (`checks.json`), one analysis repeated on two fixed cuts of the same unit. Shift is +4 CT L1 voxels along the normal (19.192 um). The CT background is unchanged.
- Row 68: mean 90.403 to 88.424, drop 1.979 native CT units (2.19%), paired n=2670.
- Row 208: mean 83.359 to 75.184, drop 8.175 native CT units (9.81%), paired n=2999.

A positive intensity drop checks sensitivity to an offset; it is not a sheet-identity gate. In an unblinded agent review the shifted line visibly departs bright-layer portions, most clearly on row 208; it is not uniformly off-layer. The smaller drop on row 68 is retained rather than hidden.
